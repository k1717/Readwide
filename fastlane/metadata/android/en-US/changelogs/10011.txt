Readwide 1.0.11
- Added basic legacy Word (.doc) formatting and improved HWP spacing.
- Expanded read-aloud to PDF, EPUB, Word, HWP/HWPX, and Markdown.
- Improved archive support for 7z PPMd/BCJ2, RAR5 header encryption, ALZ/EGG split/encrypted cases, and .zst/.lz4.
- Faster image preview in split archives.
- Fixed recent-list swipe sensitivity and several document rendering edge cases.
