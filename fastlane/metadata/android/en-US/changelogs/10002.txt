Readwide 1.0.2
- Added a Markdown WebView reader while keeping the exact TXT model for plain TXT.
- Added text-first, read-only HWP/HWPX support (Apache-2.0 hwplib/hwpxlib), grouped with Word and legacy DOC under the Word filter; DOC rendering stays unsupported.
- Unified rendered-document bookmarks (Markdown, EPUB, Word, HWP/HWPX, PDF) around content anchors plus page/position metadata.
- Tightened 7z/CB7 split/password handling; RAR/CBR scoped to unencrypted single-volume decode-only.
