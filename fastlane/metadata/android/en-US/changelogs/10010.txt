Readwide 1.0.10
- Faster solid/sequential 7z/CB7, TAR/CBT (gzip/bzip2/xz/lzma) and RAR/CBR image paging: start before full extraction, decode forward once and clear the previous image while loading.
- Retain legacy extraction fallback for RAR cases the sequential engine cannot read, including some encrypted archives.
- Smoother PDF paging during rapid taps. No new dependency; ZIP/CBZ, ALZ and EGG behavior is unchanged.
